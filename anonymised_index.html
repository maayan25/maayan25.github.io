About

I am a 2nd year fully-funded PhD student in the Department of Informatics at Anonymous London, supervised by Anonymous Anonymous and Anonymous Anonymous, and aligned with the Anonymous and Anonymous Centre for Anonymous. My research focuses on leveraging common-sense knowledge for the process of robot task planning. More specifically, it aims to improve the safety and trustworthiness of plans (sequences of actions) that are generated for a robot to execute, and be able to reach a desired goal in a given environment. This is to be done using structured knowledge bases (knowledge graphs), by incorporating knowledge which is intuitive to humans into the decision-making process during plan generation. The primary goal is to improve planning approaches, whilst making the planning process safer and more interpretable to users, and by doing so, increasing people's trust in the system.

About

I am a 2nd year fully-funded PhD student in the Department of Informatics at King's College London, supervised by Dr Gerard Canal and Dr Albert Meroño Peñuela, and aligned with the UKRI Safe and Trusted AI Centre for Doctoral Training.

My research focuses on leveraging common-sense knowledge for the process of robot task planning. More specifically, it aims to improve the safety and trustworthiness of plans (sequences of actions) that are generated for a robot to execute, and be able to reach a desired goal in a given environment.
